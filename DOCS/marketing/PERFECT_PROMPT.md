# The "Perfect" Marketing Prompt

Copy and paste the block below into an advanced AI (like ChatGPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, or DeepSeek) to get a highly tailored marketing strategy.

---

### 📋 The Prompt

> Act as a Senior Growth Marketing Lead specializing in Music/Entertainment SaaS Apps for Gen Z.
>
> I need a data-backed recommendation on the best advertising platform strategy for my app launch in **2026**.
>
> **Here is the detailed context of the Product:**
> *   **App Name:** FreeStyla
> *   **Core Function:** A freestyle rap practice ecosystem. It provides instrumental beats, synchronized word prompts, and recording tools to help users practice flow and improvisation.
> *   **Platforms:** IT is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** that is also deployed as a **Trusted Web Activity (TWA)** on the **Google Play Store**. Users can access it via Web or Android App.
> *   **Target Audience:** Aspiring rappers, hip-hop enthusiasts, vocalists, and hobbyists. Primarily Gen Z / Mobile-first users.
> *   **Business Model:** Freemium.
>     *   **Free:** Basic practice tools, read-only storage.
>     *   **Pro Subscription (€4.99/mo):** Unlimited recording, cloud upload, advanced stats, full beat library.
> *   **Unique Selling Points:** "The Duel" (Multiplayer mode), "Beat Vault" (Cloud storage), and high-fidelity "Studio" aesthetics.
>
> **The Challenge:**
> I have a limited startup budget and need to prioritize efficiency. I am considering **TikTok Ads**, **Meta Ads (Reels/Stories)**, and **Google Ads (UAC)**.
>
> **Please provide a strategic answer covering:**
>
> 1.  **Platform Ranking:** Rank the 3 platforms (TikTok vs Meta vs Google) specifically for a *Music Practice App* in 2026. Which one should I be on *first* and why?
> 2.  **Platform Strategy:** For the winning platform, what is the specific "hook" or creative angle that works for this niche? (e.g., UGC vs Polish).
> 3.  **The Budget Roadmap (Month 1 - Month 6):**
>     *   I need a realistic monthly budget progression.
>     *   Start with a "Testing/Validation" budget in Month 1.
>     *   Scale to a "Growth" budget by Month 6 (assuming positive ROAS).
>     *   Include specific dollar/euro amounts and KPI targets (CPI, CPA) I should aim for.
>
> **Constraint:** Be brutally honest about minimum effective ad spend. Do not suggest a budget if it is too low to work for the algorithm.
