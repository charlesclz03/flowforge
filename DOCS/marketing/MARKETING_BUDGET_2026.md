## 📱 Platform Breakdown

### 1. TikTok Ads
**Best for:** Brand awareness, viral potential, younger demographic (Gen Z/Alpha), "Cool factor".
*   **Strict Minimums:** TikTok enforces a **$50 USD/day** minimum at the campaign level and **$20 USD/day** per ad group. You literally cannot run ads for less.
*   **Strategy:** User Generated Content (UGC). Polished "ads" fail here. You need raw videos of people rapping using the app.
*   **Benchmarks (2025/26):**
    *   **CPM:** $6.00 - $10.00
    *   **CPI (Install):** $1.00 - $2.50 (Can be lower for aggressive viral content)

### 2. Meta Ads (Instagram & Facebook)
**Best for:** Subscriptions, retargeting, broad reach. Instagram Reels is the direct competitor to TikTok here.
*   **Minimums:** Technical minimum is $1/day, but the **Learning Phase threshold** requires ~50 conversions per week.
*   **Math:** If your Cost Per Purchase is €15, you need 50 * 15 = €750/week = **~€100/day** to optimize effectively.
*   **Strategy:** Use "Advantage+ App Campaigns". Let AI find your users.
*   **Benchmarks (2025/26):**
    *   **CPM:** $10.00 - $15.00
    *   **CPI:** $2.50 - $5.00+

### 3. Google Ads (App Compaigns)
**Best for:** High intent. Capturing people searching for "how to freestyle rap", "rap beats", "practice rap".
*   **Minimums:** Recommended daily budget is **50x your Target CPI**.
*   **Math:** If target CPI is €2.00, recommended daily budget is **€100**.
*   **Strategy:** "Universal App Campaigns" (UAC). You provide assets, Google places them on YouTube, Search, and Play Store.
*   **Benchmarks (2025/26):**
    *   **CPI:** $1.50 - $4.00
    *   **CPC:** $1.00 - $2.00

## 💰 Unit Economics (The "Napkin Math")

Your subscription price is **€4.99/mo**.

*   **LTV (Lifetime Value):** If average user stays 4 months = **€20.00**
*   **Target CAC (Cost to Acquire Customer):** You generally want LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1.
    *   **Target CAC = €6.66**
*   **Install-to-Paid Conversion:** E.g., 5% of installs become subscribers.
    *   To get 1 subscriber (€6.66 cost), you need 20 installs.
    *   **Target CPI (Cost Per Install) = €6.66 / 20 = €0.33**

> [!WARNING]
> **Reality Check:** A **€0.33 CPI** is extremely hard to achieve in Tier 1 countries (US/UK/EU).
>
> **Implication:** You may need to rely on **Organic Growth** (Viral TikToks) to lower the blended CAC, or increase your LTV (Yearly plans, higher retention).

## 🚀 Recommendation

**Start with TikTok + Organic.**
1.  **Budget:** €50/day on TikTok Ads.
2.  **Creative:** Post 1-3 organic TikToks *daily*. Boost the best performing ones with the ad budget.
3.  **Goal:** Drive cheap engagement and installs.

Do not spread thin across Google/Meta/TikTok with <€3,000/mo. Pick one battleground and win it first.
